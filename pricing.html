Why the lower cost?

Open Source Tooling

GlobeTech leverages open source security tools rather than expensive commercial platforms. This keeps costs down without sacrificing quality — the tools are customized for each engagement to deliver data that matches or exceeds commercial alternatives.

Low Overhead

No corporate office, no bloated sales team, no unnecessary infrastructure costs. GlobeTech is entirely cloud-hosted using reputable services. The savings go directly to you in the form of lower engagement costs.

Direct Access

You work directly with the person doing the testing — no project managers, no account executives, no layers between you and the technical work. This means faster communication and better results.

SMB-Focused

GlobeTech was started specifically to bring quality security assessments to small and mid-size businesses that are often priced out of the market. The barrier to entry should be low for companies looking to improve their security posture.
