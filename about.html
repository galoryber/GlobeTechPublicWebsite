About GlobeTech LLC

A Wisconsin-based penetration testing and vulnerability assessment consultancy built to serve small and mid-size businesses.

The Mission

GlobeTech LLC provides small to mid-size Wisconsin businesses with vulnerability assessments and penetration testing (VAPT) services on a project basis. The business was started to offer security assessments as a significant improvement in quality and a significant cost reduction — making professional security testing accessible to organizations that are typically priced out of the market.

This is achieved by utilizing open source security tooling rather than expensive commercial platforms. OSS infosec tools keep costs down and the barrier of entry low for internal teams. By customizing these tools for each engagement, GlobeTech delivers quality data at a lower cost than other VAPT consultants.

How We Operate

GlobeTech LLC is based out of West Bend, WI but does not maintain a corporate office. The company is entirely cloud-hosted, using reputable services like Microsoft 365, Zoho, and AWS. This lean operating model means lower overhead — and lower costs for clients.

You work directly with the person performing the assessment. No project managers, no account executives, no layers of abstraction between you and the technical work. This means faster communication, better context, and more actionable results.

The Operator

GlobeTech is run by a full-time penetration tester and red teamer with over a decade of experience in offensive security. Day-to-day work includes the automation of Purple Team frameworks for controls validation at a Fortune 500 financial services company. GlobeTech LLC operates on the side to bring that same level of expertise to small and mid-size businesses on a project basis.
