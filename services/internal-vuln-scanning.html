Internal Vulnerability Scanning

Assumed Breach assessments that go straight to identifying the risks and vulnerabilities an attacker would exploit from inside your network.

Assumed Breach Approach

Internal vulnerability scanning skips the social engineering phase and creates an "Assumed Breach" scenario by placing a scanning device directly on the network. This approach focuses time and budget on what matters most — finding the exploitable flaws inside your environment rather than testing whether a phishing email gets clicked.

What We Do

  • Deploy a scanning device on your internal network
  • Perform authenticated and unauthenticated vulnerability scanning
  • Identify misconfigurations, missing patches, and security gaps
  • Test each potential attack vector through manual penetration testing
  • Chain simple flaws together to demonstrate full Kill Chain impact
  • Assess risk and business impact of discovered vulnerabilities

Why Kill Chains Matter

Internal assessments usually uncover simple flaws that can be chained together. Often, this full "Kill Chain" of attacks shows a significantly higher impact than any individual vulnerability might suggest on its own. A medium-severity misconfiguration combined with a low-severity default credential can result in complete domain compromise. We demonstrate these chains to show real business risk.

Deliverables

The internal assessment includes:

  • Vulnerability scan reports with severity ratings
  • Supporting discovery data and asset inventory
  • Manual penetration testing results for exposed assets
  • Attack chain documentation showing how individual flaws combine
  • Prioritized mitigation recommendations

GlobeTech makes recommendations, but your business ultimately decides what's right for you. Post-engagement, we spend time together determining the actions that best fit your environment and risk tolerance.
