External Vulnerability Scanning

Assess your perimeter defense by auditing the attack surface of your externally facing assets.

Why External Assessments Matter

For many organizations, hardening their security posture starts with assessing their perimeter defense. Your internet-facing assets are the first thing an attacker sees — and often the first thing they target. GlobeTech audits the attack surface of your externally facing assets to identify the risks and vulnerabilities that an attacker would take advantage of.

Our Process

Discovery & Reconnaissance

Your internet-facing footprint is assessed by reviewing available public information and correlating it back to your systems. Benign information like public DNS records can shed light on exposed operations and services available to an attacker. This reconnaissance phase maps out the full scope of what's exposed.

Vulnerability Scanning

Discovered assets are scanned for vulnerabilities and assessed for risk and impact. The vulnerability assessment and scan report becomes one of the primary deliverables at the end of the engagement.

Penetration Testing

Each potential attack vector is reviewed and pen tested to attempt to gain access to the systems. This manual testing goes beyond what automated scanners can find, identifying business logic issues and chained vulnerabilities.

Deliverables

  • Vulnerability scan reports with severity ratings
  • Discovery and reconnaissance data
  • Manual penetration testing results
  • Prioritized mitigation recommendations

GlobeTech makes recommendations, but your business ultimately decides what's right for you. Post-engagement, we spend time together determining the actions that best fit your situation.
