What every engagement includes

Comprehensive Reporting

Up to 40% of every engagement is reserved for reporting and presentation. Each finding includes a severity score, attack narrative, proof of concept, and mitigation recommendations. The report becomes the focal point for a post-engagement discussion where each item is reviewed until the exploitation process is understood and mitigation options have been discussed.

MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

Findings are mapped to the MITRE ATT&CK framework throughout the report to identify different areas within the attack Kill Chain. This provides actionable context for your security team and helps prioritize remediation efforts based on real-world attack patterns.

Collaborative Remediation

GlobeTech makes recommendations, but your business ultimately decides what's right for you. Post-engagement, we spend time together determining the actions that best fit your risk tolerance, budget, and operational needs.

Affordable for SMBs

By leveraging open source tooling and keeping overhead low, GlobeTech delivers enterprise-quality assessments at a fraction of the cost of larger firms. No unnecessary licensing fees passed on to you.
