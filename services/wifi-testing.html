WiFi Security Testing

Wireless networks provide a local attacker with a potential access vector. We test the feasibility of unauthorized access through your 802.11 networks.

How It Works

GlobeTech sets up a device with wireless radios to identify your wireless networks and determine the encryption in place. The goal is to recover encryption keys by implementing attacks against your 802.11 networks — testing whether an attacker in range of your facility could gain unauthorized access to your corporate network.

What We Do

  • Identify all wireless networks operating within your facility
  • Determine encryption protocols and configuration
  • Scan and sniff wireless traffic to intercept encrypted communications
  • Attempt to break encryption using industry-standard and custom tooling
  • Test for rogue access points and unauthorized wireless devices
  • Evaluate network segmentation between wireless and wired networks

Customized Attacks

More than just running industry tools — each wireless engagement is taken to the next level with heavily customized attacks tailored specifically to your systems. This targeted approach provides a better assessment and higher success rate than generic scanning alone.

Certification

GlobeTech holds the OSWP (Offensive Security Wireless Professional) certification, demonstrating proven expertise in wireless penetration testing techniques and methodologies.

WiFi as Part of Network Testing

WiFi security assessments are also included as part of network penetration testing engagements. Wireless networks represent an additional attack surface that should be evaluated alongside your wired infrastructure to provide a complete picture of your security posture.
